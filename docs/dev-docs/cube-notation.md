# Cube Notation Reference

## Turn notation
- Letter alone (ex. F) = clockwise turn (as if viewing that face directly)
- Letter + apostrophe (ex. F') = counterclockwise turn, referred to as "prime" e.g. U' = U prime
- All turns = one 90° quarter turn unless explicitly stated otherwise

## Standard cube notation

U - the top (Up) face of the cube when held parallel to the ground
D - the bottom (Down) face of the cube
L - the Left face of the cube
R - the Right face of the cube
F - the Front face of the cube (facing TOWARDS the holder)
B - the Back face of the cube (facing AWAY from the holder)

## Default/Neutral Orientation (calibration & testing baseline)
White (U) facing up, Green (F) facing the user.
All calibration steps, test cases, and bug reports should assume this 
orientation unless explicitly stated otherwise.
