## BUG: Gyroscope yaw drift in gameplay scenarios.

## Description:
Inputs all functional in-game. Drift issue discovered with Yaw axis AKA camera left-right controls (currently mapped as O+P keys) where after approximately 5-10 minutes of gameplay, drift had accumulated on left side of Yaw axis, causing software to eventually read Yaw as <-30° at neutral position. This caused in-game camera to be at a constant left spin. WASD movement continued to work as intended during this, and physical turning of Yaw to counter drift worked as temporary real time fix. Resetting software and reconnecting cube at neutral position reset all gyroscope values to 0 and made controls fully functional temporarily until drift accumulated again.

## Steps to reproduce:
1. Connect Gocube to software and begin a gameplay session in Dark Souls 3
2. Use gyroscope for movement for 5-10 minutes of gameplay
3. Observe yaw value in HUD
4. Attempt forward movement after time has passed

## Expected behavior:
Continuous, uninhibited function of WASD movement and camera left-right control in long term usage scenarios.

## Actual behavior:
Yaw axis in gyroscope accumulates drift over time in the form of negative integer values, observable on gyroscope HUD. This eventually reaches a number high enough to cause camera movement to the left even at neutral position. 

## UPDATE 08/25/2025: 
Yaw drift has been discovered to not be constant. Positive (forward) pitch increases the rate at which yaw drift accumulates, and pitch also slightly increases the negative value on yaw a small amount in tandem with pitch increase. This leads to the drift-caused camera movement to occur sooner during forward movement before drift has accumulated enough to trigger camera movement at neutral position.
