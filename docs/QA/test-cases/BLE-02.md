## ID: BLE-02

## Title: Sustained Bluetooth connection test

## Preconditions:
- software running
- cube connected to software

## Steps:
1. With cube connected, do not make any face turns, leave cube at rest on desk and do not touch for 10 consecutive minutes.
2. Check console window for any disconnect or error messages during the rest period.
3. After 10 minutes, perform one face turn to confirm the connection is still functional (not just appearing connected).
4. If successful, repeat steps 1-3 as many times as desired, up to 60 minutes total. (confirm in test log how many 10min cycles were performed)

## Expected Result:
cube remains connected to software indefinitely, until cube battery dies, or software manually disconnected or stopped.

## Related Issue(s):
none
